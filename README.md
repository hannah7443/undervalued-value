# What are your dreams?

## About
What are your dreams? Who do you wish to become? How can you make a change in the world? When I was younger, I dreamed big; my imagination was limitless. I distinctly remember making wishes with pennies and tossing them into fountains at parks - and seeing hundreds of pennies glimmering back at me as I stared into the swirling water. I wanted to make this website to encapsulate the exhilarating hope I felt every time I tossed a penny into a fountain alongside thousands of other wishes.

## Value
This website functions as a capsule of dreams, reminding each user that their dreams are not lost, but are always out there for them to pursue. Dreams never truly die...
